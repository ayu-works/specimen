HOW TO PLAY

Four blocks from a page you like to a page your agent builds.

  1. 1 · SCAN

    Open the side panel on any page and click Scan this page. It can also capture mobile and the other color theme.

  2. 2 · INSPECT

    Palette with roles, contrast checks, type scale, spacing, shapes, components and a layout blueprint.

  3. 3 · GENERATE

    Pick a Prompt for Claude Code, Cursor, v0, Lovable or generic, or a DESIGN.md.

  4. 4 · PASTE

    Hand it to your agent and build. Then the fidelity check compares your build with the original.
