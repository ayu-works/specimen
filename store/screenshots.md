# Screenshots and promo images

## Requirements
- **Screenshots:** 1 to 5 images, 1280x800 or 640x400, PNG or JPEG (24-bit PNG, no alpha). 1280x800 is preferred.
- **Small promo tile (required):** 440x280.
- **Marquee promo tile (optional):** 1400x560.
- Show the real UI. Do not add misleading claims or other brands' logos.

Final shots will be built from the user's own usage screenshots.
