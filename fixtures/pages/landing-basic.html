Frequently asked questions

How long does setup take?

Most teams are collecting events within fifteen minutes using our JavaScript snippet or server SDKs.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every chart exports to CSV, and raw events can be streamed to your warehouse at any time.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

We offer 50% off any paid plan for registered nonprofits and education programmes.

Is Northwind compliant with GDPR?

Yes. Data can be hosted in the EU, and we provide a data processing agreement on request.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

We never drop data. We will email you, and you can upgrade or let the overage roll into next month.
