# Suno brief: the Specimen jingle

These lyrics are **original**. They follow the reference video's *structure* (spoken question in the cold open, shouted hook on the drop, karaoke lines, a chant section, punchline), but none of its words. Each line is timed to a scene in our video.

## How to use it
1. Open Suno → **Create** → turn on **Custom**.
2. Paste the **Style** text into "Style of Music".
3. Paste the **Lyrics** into "Lyrics". The `[Section]` tags help Suno follow the structure.
4. Title: `Specimen`.
5. Generate 4–6 takes. Pick the one where "Scan it!" hits hard right after the spoken intro and the chorus is catchy. The song will run longer than 30 s, and that's fine. I'll cut it.
6. Download it as **WAV** (MP3 also works) and put it in `design/video/audio/`. Tell me which file. I'll detect its tempo and the drop, line the cuts up with the beat, retime the karaoke words to the sung syllables, and mix our sound effects under the vocal.

## Style (30 s cut, 120 BPM)
```
upbeat kawaii J-pop jingle, chiptune 8-bit synths, 120 BPM, C major, bright playful female vocal, punchy four-on-the-floor drums, gang shouts, catchy short hook, commercial jingle
```
Exclude styles (if your Suno version has the field): `ballad, slow, sad, rock guitar, autotune heavy`.

## Style (40 s cut)
The 40 s video moves at 90 BPM with a double-time feel. A sung track can't be slowed down without sounding off, so generate this one separately:
```
energetic kawaii J-pop jingle, chiptune 8-bit synths, 90 BPM half-time with 180 BPM drum-and-bass drums, bright playful female vocal, gang shouts, catchy hook, commercial jingle
```

Use the same lyrics.
